JURISDICTION · SUBSTANTIVE RIGHTS · PROCEDURE

In-depth analysis of critical dispute points in China-related Investor-State Dispute Settlement (ISDS) cases. This section examines key legal issues including jurisdictional challenges, substantive rights protection, procedural matters, and compensation standards across the case dataset.

Jurisdictional Issues

Analysis of jurisdictional challenges and determinations in China-related ISDS cases, including treaty interpretation, consent to arbitration, and scope of investment protection.

  • Consent to arbitration mechanisms
  • Treaty scope and applicability
  • Investment definition and qualification

Substantive Rights Protection

Examination of substantive protections under Chinese investment treaties, including fair and equitable treatment, national treatment, most-favored-nation treatment, and expropriation standards.

  • Fair and equitable treatment (FET)
  • National treatment obligations
  • Most-favored-nation (MFN) treatment
  • Expropriation and compensation

Procedural Matters

Review of procedural issues arising in China-related ISDS proceedings, including transparency, third-party participation, consolidation, and enforcement mechanisms.

  • Third-party funding and participation
  • Case consolidation and coordination
  • Award enforcement and challenges

Compensation Standards

Analysis of compensation calculation methods, valuation approaches, and damages assessment in China-related ISDS cases, including market value, discounted cash flow, and other valuation techniques.

  • Valuation methodologies
  • Market value assessments
  • Discounted cash flow (DCF) analysis
  • Interest and cost allocation
